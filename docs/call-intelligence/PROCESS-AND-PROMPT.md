# Standard Processing Instructions v0.1 — PROPOSED

## Trigger
"GO — PROCESS [MEETING] CALL" with transcript or accessible source.

## Processing instructions for EVA
1. Confirm the source actually read, meeting identity, date, and whether transcript may be incomplete or imperfect.
2. Produce BLUF: 3–5 material discoveries, what changed since previous intelligence, and primary risk.
3. Extract timestamped, atomic, minimally paraphrased statements. Keep stakeholder assertions labeled REPORTED. Do not infer numbers when the transcript is uncertain.
4. Identify hypotheses separately, linking each to supporting and challenging evidence. Distinguish stakeholder opinion from analyst interpretation.
5. Build cross-call comparison: agreement, disagreement, different scope, new unknown. Never manufacture consensus.
6. Generate prioritized discovery questions and concise one-line actions. Include owners only when known; proposed tasks remain proposed.
7. Capture relationship signals only as observed statements and working preferences, not psychological diagnoses.
8. Check operational boundaries: source consent, confidentiality, AI policy, allowed destinations. Do not send emails, modify CCL systems, or publish private source content without approval.
9. Update the private Excel pilot workbook only when available, validating sheet structure, IDs, formulas and referential links before writing. Preserve previous rows and produce a change log.
10. Provide a concise call brief and exact next action. Surface evidence gaps and blockers.

## Brief template
- Meeting / purpose / source / completeness
- BLUF
- Key evidence with source pointers
- New or revised intelligence with confidence and alternatives
- Cross-call convergence and tensions
- Discovery questions
- Actions (one line each; proposed vs approved)
- Relationship/operating guidance grounded in explicit comments
- Unknowns / risks
- Workbook change summary / storage status

## Pilot acceptance tests
- Can a reader trace each key conclusion to a precise source?
- Are reported opinions separate from verified CCL facts?
- Can a hypothesis be challenged by a later conversation without rewriting history?
- Can actions be closed with outcomes?
- Does the workbook preserve IDs and references after a second/third call?
- Are all sensitive records excluded from public GitHub?
