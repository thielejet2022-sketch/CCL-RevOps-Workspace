# CCL Product × GTM Motion Matrix v0.1
Date: 2026-10-12
Mode: Offer Accepted / Pre-Start
Design status: PROPOSED. Motion assignments and metric selections are HYPOTHESIS pending validation. Public offering availability is KNOWN from the linked CCL pages; internal packaging, ownership, margins and volumes are UNKNOWN.

## Recommendation
Use one common customer-lifecycle architecture with motion-specific buying gates, measurement units and economics. Do not create one pipeline per product or treat all repeat purchases as subscription revenue.

Separate five dimensions: offering family; buyer/payer/participant; GTM motion; revenue pattern; delivery model. New acquisition, renewal, repeat purchase and expansion are lifecycle events, not necessarily separate motions. Region and sector are segmentation dimensions; split a motion only when buying process, sales effort or economics materially differ.

## Proposed motion gates and lead quality
| Motion | Evidence of fit | Commitment event | Reason codes for unsuccessful routing |
|---|---|---|---|
| M1 Enrollment/order | Appropriate participant/product, eligibility, format/date and purchase intent | Paid/confirmed enrollment or accepted order, per validated policy | Timing mismatch; eligibility; format; price; duplicate; no purchase intent |
| M2 Organizational program sale | Organizational sponsor, audience, volume, need and timing | Accepted organizational purchase/contract | No sponsor; unclear audience; timing; insufficient need; incorrect route |
| M3 Consultative solution | Organizational problem, desired impact, sponsor, buying process, scope and critical event | Signed agreement, with operational handoff separately validated | No problem fit; no sponsor; unresolved buying process; infeasible scope; capacity; timing |
| M4 Subscription/licensing | Ongoing development need, internal deployment owner, audience/use case, entitlement fit and buying process | Executed subscription/license | No deployment owner; poor use-case fit; entitlement mismatch; timing; commercial terms |

Use SPICED proportionately. A simple enrollment does not require enterprise discovery. Low intent, wrong route, future timing, poor fit and failure to follow up are different diagnoses. Capture sales rejection reasons and subsequent outcomes. No numeric qualification threshold is approved.

## Economics and common measurement rules
- Define acquisition unit before CAC: participant versus buying organization versus subscription account. Report new acquisition separately from renewal, repeat and expansion effort.
- CAC = acquisition-attributable marketing and sales cost / newly acquired units for a defined cohort and motion. State shared-cost allocation, included pursuit/design effort, unsuccessful pursuits, timing lag and exclusions.
- Measure economic recovery when cumulative customer contribution covers acquisition cost; report cash recovery separately. Agree with Finance which direct delivery/support costs and allocation basis are used. Never compare contribution computed under different bases.
- Distinguish incremental contribution from fully allocated profitability. Filling an existing cohort and adding a cohort have different economics. Report cohort break-even and capacity where relevant.
- Use observed 12/24/36-month cohort contribution and repeat-purchase rates before speculative lifetime value. Windows are proposed reporting views, not performance targets.
- Use ARR/GRR/NRR only for qualifying recurring contracts and consistent cohorts. Repeated projects/enrollments remain repeat business unless contractual recurrence is established.
- Keep bookings, scheduled delivery, recognized revenue, invoicing and cash as distinct measures. Sequence and timing depend on contract; no universal linear order is assumed.
- Avoid portfolio-wide Rule of 40 and SaaS CAC thresholds as operating targets. Portfolio views should include growth, operating surplus, cash, delivery feasibility and separately measured mission/customer outcomes.
- Track volumes, conversion and elapsed time at comparable gates. Do not pool enrollment conversion with enterprise opportunity conversion.
- Track program completion, behavior application and organizational impact separately from revenue. Participation alone does not establish impact.
- Reconcile portfolio totals without duplicating bundled components, accounts or motion-switching opportunities.

## Cross-motion relationship paths — HYPOTHESIS
Individual enrollment can create an organizational introduction; sponsored cohorts can reveal a custom need; custom engagements can lead to Passport; Passport can support separately scoped services. None is a mandatory customer progression or proven conversion path. Attribute the original acquisition once, record subsequent buying events and their selling costs, and preserve participant/payer/account distinctions.

## Implications for demand generation and trapped value
Demand quality is motion-specific. A legitimate individual enrollment inquiry may be misrouted to a custom seller. Separate creation of new demand from capturing existing intent and developing existing relationships.
For trapped value, inspect inactive sponsors, repeat cohorts, additional audiences/business units, Passport deployment and timely follow-on needs. Validate entitlement before calling usage an upsell. These are proposed tests, not verified CCL leakage.
Recommended demand views: qualified enrollments for M1; sales-accepted organizational demand for M2; problem-qualified opportunities for M3; deployment-qualified opportunities for M4. Pair with downstream contribution and outcome evidence rather than MQL volume alone.
