## Outcome and stopping point
By Day 30, produce an evidence-backed recommendation on which buying journeys need distinct motions, which commercial measures can be baselined, and the smallest joint improvement test worth running.
Four motions remain candidates: M1 enrollment/order; M2 organizational program purchase; M3 custom solution; M4 Passport subscription/licensing. No motion count is established.
Success is a useful decision packet with explicit gaps, not a complete audit, new dashboard or production configuration. Missing evidence leaves a motion DEFER or a metric unready; it does not prevent a scoped recommendation.
Days below are proposed working-day bands relative to an authorized discovery start, not employment dates or calendar commitments. Gates take precedence over dates.

## Days 1–3 — establish permission and align
Use a sponsor conversation to agree the business question, available time, priority offerings/regions, authorized sources and review forum. Identify actual custodians; role labels above are suggested perspectives, not assigned owners or assumed reporting lines.
Record: confirmed sponsor; decision owner; access approver; evidence custodian; permitted storage; reviewers; scope; exclusions; next review.
Brief each participating function on one common question: what prevents qualified demand and existing relationships from becoming feasible, valuable client outcomes?
Carry candidate-era statements as REPORTED or HYPOTHESIS. After employment begins and authorized internal evidence is available, perform the formal transition review before promoting them to operating truth.
Early Finance conversation establishes definitions and custody only; detailed economic calculation follows case tracing.

## Days 4–10 — trace buying journeys
Start with an available won and lost/no-decision case per candidate motion. Add repeat/renewal and exceptions only where they resolve a decision. Reuse cases across requests; this is qualitative selection, not a fixed quota or representative sample.
Recommended walkthrough order:
1. M1 enrollment/order and employer-reimbursement counterexample: identify participant, payer and actual buying process.
2. M2 organization-led seat commitment: test whether sponsor/procurement/administration changes the process enough to warrant a separate motion.
3. M3 custom solution: follow discovery, proposal/SOW, feasibility, scope and accepted delivery handoff; include unsuccessful pursuit effort.
4. M4 Passport: distinguish contracted recurrence, included entitlements, deployment/adoption and separately paid scope; use mature renewal evidence only if available.

Use the existing worksheet and evidence-request minimum fields. Preserve source locator/date, process version, actual milestones and missing links. Do not infer account identity from email domain or configure D365 to fit the model.
Exit: a traceable case for each available journey, role perspectives and limitations; missing journeys stay DEFER. Resolve material contradictions before asserting a motion.

## Days 11–15 — joint demand and relationship calibration
Review rejection/reroute, delayed follow-up and existing-account repeat/expansion cases together. Assess fit, readiness, routing, execution, selling, delivery and missing-data explanations separately.
For each case ask: what did Marketing know; what did the receiver accept/reject and why; what happened next; what evidence distinguishes the explanations; who can resolve the missing fact?
Inspect possible trapped value through inactive sponsors, additional audiences, repeat purchases or undeployed entitlements. These are HYPOTHESIS tests, not established leakage; included usage is not paid expansion.
Deliver a short case-decision log: evidence, competing diagnosis, confidence, unresolved question, confirmed resolution owner and next test.
Recommend KEEP/MERGE/SPLIT/DEFER by motion. Do not resolve M2 from organizational payment alone.
Exit: shared interpretation of available cases and explicitly unresolved causes. No population lead-quality or conversion conclusion from selected cases.

## Days 16–23 — economic definitions and baseline readiness
Work with Finance and Delivery to distinguish bookings, scheduled delivery, recognized revenue, invoice and cash without assuming a universal order.
Agree acquisition unit and attribution window; acquisition versus retention/expansion effort; unsuccessful pursuit/presale design; shared allocations; incremental versus allocated delivery cost; bundled versus paid scope; currency/tax/refund treatment.
Inspect capacity: filling seats in an existing cohort versus adding a cohort, design/faculty/coach constraints and estimate versus actual effort.
Define eligible repeat/renewal populations, observation windows and censored outcomes. Separate completion, application and organizational impact.
For each proposed metric record definition, unit, numerator/denominator, population/window, exclusions, sources, reconciliation, custodian, limitations and readiness:
READY TO BASELINE / DEFINITION NEEDED / DATA NEEDED / NOT YET OBSERVABLE / NOT APPLICABLE.
Then request the complete agreed population, including open/lost/cancelled/no-decision cases. Latest 12 months is a proposed starting scope; older cohorts may be required for mature outcomes.
If data are incomplete, deliver the definition and gap rather than fabricated CAC, retention, LTV or recovery precision. Avoid portfolio SaaS targets.

## Days 24–30 — review and choose one test
Decision packet:
- Motion recommendations with case references, counterexamples, evidence states and unresolved boundaries.
- Demand/relationship findings with alternative explanations; separate measured facts from hypotheses.
- Metric readiness and only those baselines supported by adequate populations and maturity.
- Source/identity/ownership gaps and the smallest next evidence action.
- One proposed pilot with business purpose, confirmed accountable owner, eligible cases, action, observation window, success/stop criteria and review date.

Possible pilots, subject to evidence: accepted rerouting with receipt confirmation; stalled qualified inquiry follow-up; existing-sponsor re-engagement; Passport deployment support. Select one only after discovery; no pilot is pre-approved.
Use actual source history and feasible comparison where available; a small pilot supports learning, not a causal claim by itself. Separate commercial outcomes from mission/client outcomes.
Review decisions with the confirmed authority. Record accept/revise/defer and rationale. No automatic pipeline, stage, scoring, automation or SIE production changes follow from this document.

## Exceptions and focus rules
| Situation | Response |
|---|---|
| Internal access unavailable | Continue preparation with sanitized/public material; no CCL operational findings; mark gates blocked |
| A candidate journey has no accessible case | DEFER it and document custodian/access gap; do not force four motions |
| Marketing and Sales disagree | Review the same case timeline and receiving-owner evidence; retain unresolved causes |
| Finance definitions or costs conflict | Reconcile bases before calculation; publish metric readiness instead of a target |
| Cohort outcomes are immature | NOT YET OBSERVABLE; preserve eligible dates and future review |
| Discovery expands | Ask which decision the extra evidence changes; stop when the decision packet supports one bounded test |
