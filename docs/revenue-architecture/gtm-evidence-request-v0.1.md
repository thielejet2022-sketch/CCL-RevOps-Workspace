Date: 2026-10-12
Mode: Candidate / pre-employment.
Status: PROPOSED discovery request for review and later authorized internal use. Not sent. No access, owners, deadlines or export capability are assumed.
Purpose: validate the four candidate motions and their lead-quality, delivery and economic measures without importing SaaS benchmarks.
References: [product-motion matrix](product-gtm-motion-matrix-v0.1.md), [worksheet v0.2](gtm-motion-validation-worksheet-v0.1.md), [synthetic findings](gtm-motion-synthetic-tests-2026-10-12.md).

## Request brief
Please help us trace a small selection of actual purchases from initial demand through delivery and the next customer decision. We want to understand who bought, how the purchase happened, what was promised, what it took to deliver, and what happened afterward. Existing reports and guided record walkthroughs are sufficient to start; a new dashboard or broad system export is not required.
The aim is to decide which journeys need distinct GTM motions, distinguish demand-fit problems from routing/follow-up/capacity issues, and agree which economic measures are meaningful for each motion.

## Stage 1 — minimum discovery packet
Choose recent cases under the current process, stating dates and any process changes. Start with one won and one lost/no-decision case for each candidate where available; add repeat/renewal and routing/capacity exceptions. Employer reimbursement and organization-led seat purchases should both be included. This is purposive qualitative discovery, not a representative sample or benchmark. Reuse cross-cutting cases; do not insist on a fixed count or treat missing cases as zero activity.

| Request ID | Candidate motion | Minimum evidence | Question resolved |
|---|---|---|---|
| E1 | M1 Enrollment/order | Standard order/enrollment and any assistance history; participant/payer roles; dates, eligibility, cancellation/attendance; reimbursement counterexample | Does organizational payment actually change the buying process? |
| E2 | M2 Organizational program purchase | Sponsored seat/volume purchase; sponsor, procurement and scheduling trail; terms; seller effort if available; repeat purchase | Is this distinct from individual enrollment or a custom solution? |
| E3 | M3 Custom solution | Won and unsuccessful pursuit; discovery/proposal/SOW; design estimate and effort; feasibility approval; accepted delivery handoff; scope changes | What differentiates consultative selling, and where do scope/capacity constrain it? |
| E4 | M4 Passport | Executed agreement and recurring/nonrecurring lines; tier entitlements; deployment plan; admin/learner usage; support/customization; renewal or eligible lapse | What is subscription, included service, activation and paid expansion? |
| E5 | Cross-motion demand | One rejection/reroute and one delayed-follow-up case; initial/final route, reasons, accepting owner, subsequent outcome | Was the problem poor fit, timing, routing, execution or capacity? |
| E6 | Cross-motion relationships | Existing-account repeat or expansion buying event; original and later purchase references; sponsor/business unit links | Where can relationship value be developed without counting it as new acquisition? |

Suggested source perspectives, with actual custody/authority UNKNOWN: marketing for inquiry/route; sales/enrollment team for purchase journey; delivery/product/client success for scope, capacity and usage; Finance for contract/cost/revenue/cash; system/report owner for record linkage. Confirm who holds and may share evidence before assigning requests.

### Minimum case fields
Use existing identifiers and labels; do not create production fields during discovery.
- Case reference; source locator; observation dates; offering and region/channel; lifecycle event (new/repeat/renewal/expansion); known process version.
- Initial candidate motion, final route, reason for change and acceptance date.
- Buyer, approver, payer, participant and organizational linkage; record uncertainty rather than matching by email domain alone.
- Inquiry, acceptance, commitment/no-decision, handoff and delivery/activation dates where applicable.
- Scope/contract reference; bundled components and entitlements; promised versus delivered scope.
- Rejection, delay, cancellation or capacity reason and supporting evidence; subsequent outcome.
- Responsible roles, actual handoff recipient and known approval authority.
- Repeat/renewal eligibility, observation window and outcome; enablement, use and impact measured separately.
Use source dates rather than inventing missing milestones. Allow NOT APPLICABLE, UNKNOWN and NOT YET OBSERVABLE.

## Stage 2 — economics and measurement packet
Begin after journeys and acquisition units are understood. Use existing definitions and reports first; disclose gaps.
| Request ID | Evidence | Required distinctions | Initial output |
|---|---|---|---|
| E7 | Finance definitions, line items and contract terms | Bookings, scheduled delivery, recognized revenue, invoices, cash; recurring versus one-time; credits/refunds; currency/tax basis | Agreed revenue and cash definitions |
| E8 | Marketing/sales expense, unsuccessful pursuit and presale design effort | Acquisition versus retention/expansion; shared allocations; timing lag; double-count exclusions; source reliability | CAC definition and readiness, not target |
| E9 | Delivery/support costs and effort | Incremental cost versus allocated fixed cost; included scope versus approved additional service; cohort/resource cost | Comparable contribution views |
| E10 | Capacity and scheduling | Filling an existing seat versus creating a cohort; faculty/coach/design bottlenecks; estimate versus actual effort | Capacity-aware economics and feasibility |
| E11 | Repeat/renewal/expansion histories | Eligible cohorts and windows; contracted recurrence versus repeat projects; bundled revenue; censoring and currency | Retention/relationship definitions and readiness |
| E12 | Client outcomes | Completion, application/behavior change and organizational impact; method, respondent/audience and observation date | Outcome evidence, with limitations |

For later baselines, request a complete defined population over an agreed time window, including open, lost, cancelled and no-decision records—not just successes. A proposed starting window is the latest 12 months; use earlier cohorts when renewal or repeat observation needs longer history and record process changes. This is a proposed scope, not a known CCL data availability. Agree period, coverage, granularity, cost allocations and sufficient maturity before calculation. Stage-1 case samples cannot establish portfolio CAC, conversion rates, LTV or retention benchmarks.
If customer-level pursuit costs are unavailable, use Finance-approved aggregate/cohort allocation with limitations; do not fabricate precision.
Economic recovery can be modeled only with stated contribution/timing assumptions. Cash recovery needs receipt and outflow timing. Upfront receipts alone are insufficient.

## Review and acceptance
1. Check source, dates, case coverage, identity links and cost/contract definitions.
2. Complete worksheet cards and traces; list missing evidence and contradictions.
3. Produce KEEP/MERGE/SPLIT/DEFER recommendations for motions, with counterexamples and confirmed decision authority.
4. Classify metrics READY TO BASELINE / DEFINITION NEEDED / DATA NEEDED / NOT YET OBSERVABLE / NOT APPLICABLE.
5. Separate demand-fit, timing, routing, follow-up and capacity diagnoses; describe confidence and alternative explanations.
6. Recommend the smallest next test. Do not implement stages, scoring or automation from these examples.

Minimum useful completion: each available candidate journey has a traceable case, contract/commitment evidence, identified role perspectives and explicit limitations; missing journeys stay DEFER. Economic analysis additionally requires agreed cost/revenue bases and adequate cohort coverage. No numeric success threshold or CCL performance target is set here.
