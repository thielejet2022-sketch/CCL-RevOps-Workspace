## Purpose and decision
Determine whether weak conversion arises from targeting, readiness, incorrect routing, handoff execution, selling, delivery feasibility or missing evidence. Identify specific recoverable demand without treating every dormant relationship as recoverable pipeline.
Shared objective: qualified demand that progresses to commercially and operationally viable commitments and client impact.

## 2. Motion-specific qualification
| Candidate motion | Evidence needed for a proportionate handoff | Conversion endpoint |
|---|---|---|
| M1 Enrollment/order | Participant/product fit, eligibility, format/date, purchase interest; distinguish employer payer from participant | Confirmed enrollment/accepted order under validated policy |
| M2 Organizational program purchase | Sponsor, audience, relevant need, likely volume and timing | Validated organizational opportunity, then purchase |
| M3 Custom solution | Organizational problem, desired impact, sponsor access and willingness to explore; buying process, critical event and delivery feasibility developed in discovery | Problem-qualified opportunity, then scoped commitment |
| M4 Subscription/licensing | Ongoing use case, relevant audience, deployment ownership and entitlement fit; buying process developed in discovery | Deployment-qualified opportunity, then executed agreement |

A download or webinar attendance establishes engagement, not buying intent. Do not require full enterprise SPICED discovery for an enrollment or before every Marketing handoff.
For organizational pursuits, Marketing can capture Situation and possible Pain; discovery validates Impact, Critical Event and Decision. Each element may be known, reported or unknown.

## 4. Disposition and diagnosis dictionary
Use one current disposition plus primary and contributing reason codes; retain history rather than overwriting earlier decisions. Capture stage-entry/disposition timestamps and observation cutoff. Report ever-rerouted incidence separately from current reroute status. Duplicate/linked is a source-record status: linked duplicates do not add buying events or enter unique-event disposition percentages; retain duplicate-record rates separately.
| Disposition | Meaning | Required next action |
|---|---|---|
| Accept | Suitable for the identified sales/service motion | Owner, first action and dated next step |
| Recycle | Relevant, but not ready for that motion | Specific nurture purpose and trigger/review date |
| Reroute | Legitimate demand assigned to wrong motion/owner | Transfer, receiving owner confirmation and context |
| Reject | Evidence supports unsuitable demand | Structured reason and evidence |
| Pending/unworked | Qualification or follow-up not completed | Assign owner and resolve; do not label bad quality |
| Duplicate/linked | Existing inquiry, account or opportunity | Resolve identity and link; preserve source history |

Reason codes:
- FIT: wrong audience, eligibility, geography/serviceability, need/offering mismatch.
- READINESS: education only, future timing, no active initiative, sponsor/buying access not yet established.
- ROUTING: wrong motion, territory or owner; existing relationship not recognized.
- EXECUTION: unassigned, no response, delayed response, agreed attempts not completed, missing next step.
- SELLING: discovery gap, unresolved decision process, unresolved objection, competitor loss, no decision.
- DELIVERY/COMMERCIAL: infeasible date/capacity, scope or entitlement mismatch, commercial terms.
- DATA: missing outcome, inconsistent stage use, identity unresolved, source attribution missing.
“No sponsor yet” or “no response” alone does not establish poor fit. Seller rejection is evidence of seller judgment, not independent truth.

## 5. Measurement contract
Every published metric needs unit, numerator, denominator, cohort entry, observation window, inclusion/exclusion rules, data source, accountable owner and coverage.
| Measure | Definition | Interpretation |
|---|---|---|
| Target-account engagement | Unique target accounts meeting agreed engagement definition / eligible target accounts | Reach within desired market; anonymous activity reported separately |
| Qualified handoff volume | Unique eligible buying events passing motion-specific evidence gate | Supply of relevant demand; disclose unknown gate evidence |
| Acceptance / recycle / reject / reroute / pending mix | Each current disposition / all eligible handoffs in the same cohort | Compare together; acceptance alone can be gamed |
| Fit assessment coverage | Handoffs with evidence-based fit determination / eligible handoffs | Reliability of fit rate |
| Confirmed fit rate | Confirmed-fit handoffs / assessed handoffs | Report assessed and unknown counts alongside |
| Response compliance | Compliant / eligible due handoffs with an agreed standard; show noncompliant and unknown shares and evidence coverage | Separates execution from quality |
| Qualified-opportunity conversion | Unique handoff buying events producing validated opportunities / eligible handoff buying events | Organizational motions; one event counted once |
| Enrollment/order conversion | Buying events producing confirmed enrollment/order / eligible enrollment/order buying events | M1; separate participant and payer measures where needed |
| Progression and win rate | Events reaching next gate / eligible events entering prior gate; won / resolved opportunities (won + lost + closed/no-decision) | Also show open cohort count and maturity |
| Velocity | Median and upper-quartile elapsed time between specified events | Show still-open aging separately; define clock and pauses |
| Qualified pipeline created | Unduplicated initial qualified opportunity value; record later value changes separately | State valuation basis and confidence; not revenue |
| Acquisition efficiency | Attributable acquisition spend / new acquired units; optionally spend / qualified demand units | Distinguish CAC from cost per qualified demand; include cost boundaries and lag |
| Bookings and downstream economics | Commitments under validated definition; contribution under Finance-approved cost basis | Keep delivery, revenue, invoice and cash measures separate |

Compare equal-maturity cohorts by offering/motion, region, lifecycle type and source. Deduplicate buying events and account identities; multiple contacts do not create multiple organizational opportunities. Motion changes retain lineage and are not counted as fresh demand.
Do not pool individual enrollment with custom opportunity conversion. Report counts, missingness and uncertainty. Targets and follow-up time standards remain UNKNOWN until agreed and supported by evidence. Cases without an agreed response standard are not assessable for SLA compliance and must be disclosed separately. For eligible due cases, unknown response evidence is neither compliant nor a verified breach. If reporting compliance among assessed cases, also show assessment coverage. Report won/created-to-date separately from won/resolved and show open counts.
Marketing-sourced and marketing-influenced are separate attribution views; influenced revenue is not incremental causal revenue. Attribution cannot prove campaign lift. Use controlled/holdout tests where feasible.

## 6. Trapped-value review
| Population | Evidence to test | Proposed intervention | Outcome |
|---|---|---|---|
| Dormant relevant inquiries | Previous fit/need plus renewed trigger or readiness | Context-specific reactivation | Conversation → qualified buying event → commitment |
| Closed/no-decision pursuits | Reason resolved or conditions changed | Revisit original problem with seller | Reopened qualified opportunity; retain original lineage |
| Active stalled opportunities | Identified obstacle and missing stakeholder/proof | Seller-coordinated education or proof | Obstacle resolved, agreed next step, progression |
| Existing clients | Validated impact, adjacent need, sponsor/access | Account-specific development | New qualified need and paid commitment |
| Subscription deployment gaps | Entitlement, owner, adoption and unresolved needs | Enablement or service recovery | Adoption/impact; expansion only if separately paid and valid |

For each population capture eligible count, cases worked, conversations, qualified events, commitments, contribution where measurable, effort cost and elapsed time. Unworked eligible cases remain in population reporting; report worked-case conversion separately.
Exclude duplicate, obsolete, unsuitable and consent-restricted cases with reasons. Verify contact permissions and current relationship ownership before outreach.
Do not sum overlapping trapped-value buckets, value contracted entitlements as upsell, or estimate recoverable pipeline without verified need and value. Existing-account development does not replace new-account acquisition.

## 7. Joint diagnostic workshop
Proposed sequence:
1. Assemble a stratified sample across motions, sources, regions and dispositions, including won, lost, pending, recycled and dormant cases. Inspect the population before choosing sample sizes.
2. Marketing and Sales independently classify cases using the same evidence card.
3. Reconcile disagreement: definition issue, missing evidence or true judgment difference?
4. Trace comparable cohorts to downstream outcomes and follow-up compliance.
5. Choose one test with a named owner and observable result; avoid simultaneous changes that prevent learning.

Sampling caveat: a balanced diagnostic sample is not representative of portfolio performance. Compute population rates from complete eligible cohorts, or use a documented sampling design and weights. Small samples diagnose patterns; they do not establish targets.
