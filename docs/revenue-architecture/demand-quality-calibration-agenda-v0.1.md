## Repeatable case discussion
1. State buying event, candidate motion, lifecycle type and identity confidence. Organizational payment alone does not establish M2.
2. Read timeline and original seller judgment without silently rewriting history.
3. Compare independent fit/readiness/routing/execution assessments: Yes / No / UNKNOWN; confidence separately.
4. State current disposition: accept, recycle, reroute, reject or pending/unworked. Duplicate/linked is a source-record status, not another buying event.
5. Choose primary and contributing diagnoses using FIT, READINESS, ROUTING, EXECUTION, SELLING, DELIVERY/COMMERCIAL, DATA; use UNRESOLVED when evidence cannot select a cause.
6. Identify observable next action, accepting recipient, missing fact and review trigger. Do not assign an absent owner.

## Questions that resolve common disagreements
| Claim | Evidence question | Permissible conclusion |
|---|---|---|
| “Sales rejected it, so it was poor quality.” | Which motion-specific fit criterion failed, and what source supports that? | Seller judgment is REPORTED; evidence may support rejection, reroute or unresolved diagnosis |
| “They did not respond.” | Is outreach documented? Were attempts and timing governed by an agreed standard? | Separate no outreach, unsuccessful contact and missing activity evidence |
| “The download is a qualified lead.” | Is there a relevant request/problem and readiness for the receiving motion? | Engagement alone does not establish buying intent; purposeful recycle may be appropriate |
| “We lost revenue because follow-up was slow.” | Is loss and causal linkage supported, or only delay observed? | Execution gap can be established without attributing revenue loss |
| “This account is trapped value.” | What current need, sponsor, trigger, ownership and permissible contact support action? | Hypothesis to investigate; no recoverable pipeline amount without verified basis |
| “More Passport usage is expansion.” | Is usage entitled, or is there a separately paid scope/commitment? | Adoption/service value differs from paid expansion |
| “Conversion is improving.” | Are units, maturity, eligibility, duplicates and windows comparable? | Population/cohort evidence required; case discussion alone is insufficient |

## Measurement guardrails
Do not calculate portfolio conversion, CAC, recovery or retention from this selected case set.
For later measurement, agree unit, numerator/denominator, entry/window/cutoff, exclusions, source, owner and coverage. Classify READY TO BASELINE / DEFINITION NEEDED / DATA NEEDED / NOT YET OBSERVABLE / NOT APPLICABLE.
Deduplicate buying events; retain reroute lineage and distinguish ever-rerouted from current disposition. Keep M1 enrollment outcomes separate from organizational opportunity outcomes.
Follow-up compliance requires an agreed standard: compliant / verified noncompliant / unknown, with assessment coverage. Cases lacking a standard are separately not assessable.
Win rate includes won, lost and closed/no-decision in resolved denominator; show open counts and maturity. Pipeline, bookings, delivery, revenue, invoice and cash remain distinct.
Existing-account development and new acquisition remain separate; overlapping relationship populations are not additive.
