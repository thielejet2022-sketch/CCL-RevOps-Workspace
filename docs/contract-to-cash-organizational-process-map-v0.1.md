# CCL Contract-to-Cash Organizational / Process Map v0.1

**Date:** 2026-10-07  
**Mode:** Candidate / pre-employment  
**Purpose:** Consolidate current evidence about how CCL moves from demand and opportunity creation through contract, solution preparation, delivery, invoicing, cash, and revenue recognition.

> This is an evidence map, not a validated CCL process design or organization chart. Gaps are intentionally preserved.

## Evidence states

- **REPORTED** — stated by a CCL stakeholder.
- **PUBLIC PROFILE EVIDENCE** — supplied from a public professional profile.
- **JOB DESCRIPTION** — stated in the Director, Revenue Operations posting.
- **HYPOTHESIS** — plausible interpretation requiring validation.
- **UNKNOWN** — evidence is not yet sufficient.

## BLUF

The current evidence shows that CCL's revenue lifecycle does **not** end at Closed Won. Contract creates a promise, but recognized revenue depends on downstream design, implementation, resource scheduling, and delivery. Revenue Operations is therefore likely to need visibility across both the commercial funnel and the post-contract execution funnel.

## Working lifecycle

```text
MARKETING / SALES
Demand & Lead Generation
        ↓
Lead Qualification / Opportunity Creation
        ↓
Opportunity Progression / Conversion
        ↓
Contract / Closed Won
        │
        │  Sales → Operations handoff
        ▼
DELIVERY / OPERATIONS
Solution Design
        ↓
Implementation / Project Setup
        ↓
Resource Assignment / Scheduling
        ↓
Program / Coaching / Solution Delivery
        ▼
FINANCE
Invoice
        ↓
Cash
        ↓
Revenue Recognition*
```

*Recognition timing varies by CCL offering and performance obligation. Some revenue may be recognized at delivery, some as coaching occurs, and some licensing revenue over a multi-year access period.

## Revenue timing by offering

Current evidence from David Moore:

| Offering | Observed recognition logic |
|---|---|
| Open enrollment | Revenue recognized after participant completes the program / obligation is met |
| Custom solutions | Contract may precede delivery substantially; revenue recognized when the relevant program obligation is completed |
| Coaching | Revenue recognized as coaching occurs |
| Licensing | May be recognized upfront when obligations are complete, or over 2–3 years where ongoing digital-content access is provided |

This creates at least three distinct forecasting lenses:

**Commercial forecast → Scheduled delivery forecast → Recognized revenue forecast**

A single Closed-Won number cannot represent all three.

## Critical handoffs

### 1. Marketing → Sales
Need consistent ICP, lead quality, attribution, qualification, and feedback loops.

### 2. Sales → Design / Operations
The job description explicitly makes RevOps responsible for helping ensure accurate and timely transfer of statement of work, contract, and project data into CCL project-management systems.

### 3. Design → Implementation / Resourcing
**UNKNOWN.** We know these capabilities exist, but not the actual sequence, ownership, entry/exit criteria, or systems.

### 4. Resourcing → Delivery
LSP Team Manager evidence introduces a concrete resource-management layer. David's description of the adjunct pool suggests CCL can flex delivery capacity seasonally.

### 5. Delivery → Finance
Completion/progress against the performance obligation determines invoice and/or recognition timing depending on the offering.

## Candidate-stage signal opportunities

These are **HYPOTHESES**, not current CCL signals:

- Contract signed but design not initiated within expected time
- Design complete but implementation setup incomplete
- Delivery date not established
- LSP/faculty resource not assigned
- Required delivery capacity unavailable in target recognition period
- Client reschedules contracted program
- Implementation Manager workload threatens scheduled delivery
- Contract value is Closed Won but expected recognition date moves across fiscal-year boundary
- Delivery milestone slips without corresponding forecast update
- Adjunct availability changes capacity confidence
- Required SOW/contract/project fields missing at Sales→Operations handoff

These could eventually help distinguish **bookings confidence** from **delivery confidence** and **revenue-recognition confidence**.

## RevOps design implication

The strongest current conclusion is structural:

> **CCL RevOps cannot be modeled as Marketing → Sales → Closed Won.**

The evidence supports a broader operating model:

**Demand → Opportunity → Contract → Design → Implementation → Resource Readiness → Delivery → Financial Outcome**

That does **not** mean RevOps owns every downstream function. It means reliable forecasting and commercial intelligence require RevOps to connect the data, definitions, handoffs, and signals across them.

## Candidate-stage boundary

Do not convert this map into production stages, CRM fields, workflow automation, scoring weights, or ownership rules until internal evidence validates the actual CCL process and systems.
