# CCL Design and Delivery Discovery Checklist

Date: 2026-10-08
Status: PROPOSED, candidate/pre-employment
Basis: Bill Adams interview, October 8; SIE taxonomy v0.2 reconciliation.

## Passport scope
1. What customization and design support is included in each Passport tier?
2. How are design requests estimated and tracked?
3. Who approves exceptions to agreed scope?

## LSP allocation
1. When and how is an LSP assigned to a sales opportunity?
2. How are pre-sale, Passport and custom-program workloads balanced?
3. Who decides when two commitments need the same LSP?

## Delivery handoff
1. What triggers assignment of an Implementation Manager?
2. What information must pass from Sales and LSP to Operations?
3. How do scheduling changes reach Finance?

## Evidence needed
Validate with responsible owners, documented rules, system sources and representative outcomes. The interview observations are REPORTED, not independently verified. No production thresholds, signal IDs, CRM fields or automations are authorized.

## Recommended first question
When Passport customization and a new enterprise pursuit compete for the same LSP time, how is the decision made and who owns it?
