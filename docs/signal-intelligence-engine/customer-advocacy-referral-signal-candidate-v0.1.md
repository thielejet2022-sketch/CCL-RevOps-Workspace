# Customer Advocacy & Referral Signal v0.1
Date: 2026-10-11
Status: HYPOTHESIS, candidate-stage discovery only. Does not change SIE v0.2 taxonomy (7 domains, 21 signals).

## Purpose
Capture publicly expressed customer satisfaction as evidence for potential references, referrals and expansion. Public endorsement is NOT permission to request an introduction or claim a customer is referral-ready.

## Proposed event
PUBLIC_CUSTOMER_ADVOCACY_MENTION (provisional label only).
Capture original URL, author, organization, date, verbatim claim or faithful summary, program, claimed result, evidence verification, possible account match, and review status. No production CRM fields yet.

## Decision contract
What changed: customer-authored or customer-published positive mention.
Why: third-party proof for enterprise custom programs and possible account development.
Evidence: attributable original post.
Data confidence: unknown until source verified.
Interpretation confidence: low for referral willingness without explicit consent.
Missing: actual account relationship, contract, owner, program outcomes, reuse rights, customer preferences.
Action: account owner considers a thank-you and permission-based reference/referral conversation.
Owner: UNKNOWN until internal validation.
Outcome: record whether permission, introduction, opportunity, or decline occurs.

## Workflow
OBSERVE → INVESTIGATE → RECOMMEND → LEARN (proposed, not current CCL workflow).
Human review only. No automated outreach, weights, scoring, thresholds, attribution claims, or changes to approved architecture.
