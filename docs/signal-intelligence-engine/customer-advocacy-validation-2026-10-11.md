## Evidence vs inference
KNOWN (publicly sourced): posts, program descriptions, reported result and awards.
UNKNOWN: current CRM account IDs, commercial relationship status, contract amounts, account owner, permissions, advocacy policies, referral willingness.
HYPOTHESIS: public advocacy may enable case studies, warm introductions, competitive references or expansion.
Do not infer referral readiness or generate automated outreach.

## Candidate decision-contract application
