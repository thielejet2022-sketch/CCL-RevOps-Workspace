**Purpose:** Capture evidence-backed delivery roles and handoffs relevant to Revenue Operations, forecasting, capacity, and the client lifecycle.

**Status:** Candidate / pre-employment research. This is not a complete CCL organization chart.

## Evidence classification

- **REPORTED** — stated by a CCL stakeholder in an interview.
- **PUBLIC PROFILE EVIDENCE** — role/title and description supplied from a public LinkedIn profile.
- **UNKNOWN** — not yet validated.

## Emerging delivery model

Current evidence suggests the following **working model**, not yet a validated org chart:

**Contract / Closed Won → Solution Design → Implementation / Project Setup → Faculty / LSP Resourcing → Delivery → Invoice → Cash / Revenue Recognition**

Important nuance: an **LSP may span multiple parts of this chain**. Bill Adam's profile describes an LSP as a client-serving role and his later LSP Team Manager role explicitly includes resourcing and people management. David Moore also described LSPs as part of the faculty delivery model. We should therefore avoid prematurely modeling LSP as only a trainer/instructor.

## RevOps / SIE implications

Potential signals and operating questions created by this evidence:

1. **Resource readiness:** Is the required LSP/faculty capacity identified for contracted work?
2. **Implementation readiness:** Has the work cleared operational setup prerequisites?
3. **Design readiness:** Is solution design sufficiently complete to schedule delivery?
4. **Capacity risk:** Are LSP teams, adjunct pools, or Implementation Managers becoming constrained in a period?
5. **Schedule confidence:** Does staffing/resource assignment increase confidence that contracted revenue will be delivered and recognized in the forecast period?
6. **Handoff quality:** Is the Sales → Design/Operations handoff complete enough to avoid downstream delay?

These are hypotheses for the CCL Signal Intelligence Engine until internal process/data evidence validates them.

## Open questions
